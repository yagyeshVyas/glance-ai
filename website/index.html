08Questions

The fine print,
up front.

Is Glance really free?

Yes. The app is MIT licensed and there is no account or subscription. You pay only your model provider for the calls you make, and you can point it at a local model to pay nothing at all.

Can a proctoring tool detect it?

It can if it is purpose-built to inspect window affinity and find the host. Glance removes the pixels from ordinary screen capture and hides the window from enumeration, but the 0x11 affinity flag still exists. That flag is the mechanism. Treat â€œinvisible to a screen shareâ€ and â€œinvisible to an instrumented machineâ€ as different claims.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere except the API endpoint you configure. Screenshots, transcript, history and keys live locally; keys and stored data are encrypted with Windows DPAPI. Nothing is uploaded until there is a request to send.

Does it work on macOS?

No. Glance is Windows 10 2004+ and Windows 11, x64 or arm64. The capture-exclusion mechanism and the native cloak addon are Windows-specific.

What model should I use?

Any chat model behind an OpenAI-compatible base URL. For screen questions it needs vision; for voice it needs a Whisper-compatible transcription endpoint. A backup provider can take over if the first fails mid-call.

Should I use this in every interview?

Follow the rules of the room. Some interviews, exams and assessments prohibit assistance; using Glance there may breach a policy or an agreement you signed. The tool is honest about what it can and cannot hide. The decision to use it is yours.

09Releases Every version, and what changed in it

Ten releases,
all of it on the record.

Each line below is the released changelog, condensed. The full text ships in the repository, so a claim about a version can be checked against the version.

  1. 3.3.3 16 Nov 2026 Latest
    • Live captions: starts listening on launch and cuts audio into 1.5 s chunks — a line lands while the sentence is still being spoken.
    • Resumes on the next launch; works offline against the local Whisper server with no API key.
    • The local worker collapses Whisper's repeated-sentence loops before they reach the transcript.
  2. 3.3.2 16 Nov 2026
    • One model for audio and chat: leave the transcribe model blank and it uses the chat model, same URL and key.
    • Both audio sources are selected by default, so the transcript carries You: and Them: without touching the dropdown.
    • Fast mode is on at boot — 4 s chunks, 1.2 s question detection.
  3. 3.3.1 16 Nov 2026
    • Compositor hardening: empty-surface retry, alpha encoding sniffed instead of assumed, opaque-row fast path.
    • A window capture aimed at the panel now composites instead of returning an empty frame.
    • probe:composite samples the affinity flag 300 times across two captures and requires 0x11 throughout.
  4. 3.3.0 16 Nov 2026
    • The screenshot stops exposing the panel: the capture flag never drops, and the panel is composited back from the renderer.
    • Neutral publisher in the Authenticode signature; the installer removes the retired root on upgrade.
    • The author name is rewritten out of every commit, not just the working tree.
  5. 3.2.0 15 Nov 2026
    • User-mode hardening: PEB command line and image path, loader lists, ETW writers, and a process DACL that denies VM_READ.
    • The detector watch polls in-process every 300 ms instead of spawning tasklist every four seconds.
  6. 3.1.1 15 Nov 2026
    • Chromium's window-class literals are patched inside the signed payload, so child enumeration no longer says Electron.
  7. 3.1.0 14 Nov 2026
    • Detector-aware stealth: the affinity flag drops and the text scrambles while a known inspector is running.
    • NVIDIA Broadcast eye-contact control, plus eye-HUD landmark and blink fixes.
  8. 3.0.1 4 Nov 2026
    • Capture protection no longer comes back off after a hide/show cycle: stealth is re-asserted in both directions.
  9. 3.0.0 3 Nov 2026
    • Stealth report: every tell measured on the machine you are actually on, one row each.
    • Panic hide on Ctrl+Alt+X, meeting detection, offline OCR and an offline knowledge base.
  10. 2.0.0 2 Nov 2026
    • Ghost mode is on by default and enforced in the main process, not only by the settings checkboxes.
    • The ghost check button and the getAffinity addon export that makes its verdict readable.
  11. 1.0.2 28 Oct 2026
    • Shipped capture-ab.js: an A/B that proves the exclusion per capture path instead of asserting it.
  12. 1.0.1 28 Oct 2026
    • The overlay is reparented under a plain WinUI host class, and the exclusion flag moves to that host.
    • The native addon actually reaches the package, which earlier builds silently missed.
  13. 1.0.0 27 Oct 2026
    • First release: display-affinity exclusion, one OS identity, a self-signed installer.
